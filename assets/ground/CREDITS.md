# Ground materials

These 512px color maps are downsized ambientCG materials, released under
[CC0 1.0](https://ambientcg.com/license). They were obtained from the ambientCG
packs mirrored in [fps-buildings-env-kit](https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-buildings-env-kit/tree/main/environment/ground-textures).

| File | Original material |
| --- | --- |
| grass.jpg | [Grass004](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Grass004) |
| pavers.jpg | [Bricks066](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Bricks066) |
| road.jpg | [Road001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Road001) |
| concrete.jpg | [Concrete012](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Concrete012) |
| soil.jpg | [Ground037](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037) |

Paving joints, hexagonal Battery/BPC pavers, surface variation and blade meshes
are generated by Birdway. These materials approximate surfaces; they are not
photographs of the specific mapped location.


The additional `grass-albedo.jpg`, `grass-normal.jpg`, `grass-roughness.jpg`,
and `grass-height.png` are the 1k Leafy Grass PBR maps by Charlotte Baglioni,
from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/leafy_grass), released under
[CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). JPEG maps were recompressed locally;
normal, roughness, and height maps use linear color space.

`water-normal.jpg` comes from the [Three.js r180 ocean example](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/r180/examples/webgl_shaders_ocean.html).
Three.js is MIT licensed; see `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`. It supplies small ripples
in addition to Birdway's original directional river waves.
