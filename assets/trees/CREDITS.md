# Tree textures

The leaf atlas PNGs are from [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree),
by Daniel Greenheck, under the MIT license included in
[`EZ-TREE-LICENSE.txt`](./EZ-TREE-LICENSE.txt). The atlas images are used on
individual three-dimensional foliage cards; they are not bundled tree models.

The oak bark color, normal, and roughness maps are from the EZ-Tree package and
derive from [Poly Haven's Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02),
released under CC0. The maps are included here at 1K resolution.

The close pine model in `polyhaven/` is a trimmed, single-tree copy of the 1K
glTF LOD0 from [Pine Tree 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/pine_tree_01) by Rob
Tuytel and Rico Cilliers, released under CC0. It retains the model's PBR bark,
trunk and twig maps. The bundled copy keeps one of the source's three tree
variants and excludes the two unused variants and higher-detail geometry.
