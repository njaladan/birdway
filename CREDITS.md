# Sources, credits and accuracy

Birdway's application, architectural shaders, ambient music and street props are original. The game uses bundled data and assets; it does not contact a map or asset service at runtime.

- **Building footprints and baseline heights:** NYC Open Data, Building Footprints (`5zhs-2jue`), using the snapshot redistributed by [anchitjaincfa/manhattan-3d](https://github.com/anchitjaincfa/manhattan-3d). See the [NYC dataset](https://data.cityofnewyork.us/City-Government/Building-Footprints/5zhs-2jue) and [official metadata](https://github.com/CityOfNewYork/nyc-geo-metadata/blob/main/Metadata/Metadata_BuildingFootprints.md). The playable subset contains 20,868 footprints, quantized to about one metre. Heights supplied in feet were converted to metres; missing heights use a 4 m fallback.
- **Surveyed roof geometry:** 19,666 footprints are matched to the 2014 NYC 3D Building Model LOD2 roof geometry distributed in [rolyatmax/nyc-buildings](https://github.com/rolyatmax/nyc-buildings). The match uses location and dimensions; it keeps the newer footprint and fallback roof height when a reliable survey match is unavailable. The repository's model data is used, not its application code.
- **Building age and class:** Year-built and building-class fields come from the 2017 MapPLUTO dataset published by the NYC Department of City Planning. These fields select among eight procedural façade styles; the façades are generated approximations, not source imagery or surveyed façade models. See [NYC Planning's PLUTO data page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/planning/data-maps/open-data/dwn-pluto-mappluto.page).
- **Parks, plazas, paths and trees:** Derived from the Manhattan OpenStreetMap PBF extracts at [urban-toolkit/autark: lower_mnt.osm.pbf](https://github.com/urban-toolkit/autark/blob/main/gallery/public/data/lower_mnt.osm.pbf) and [urban-toolkit/autark: mnt.osm.pbf](https://github.com/urban-toolkit/autark/blob/main/usecases/public/data/mnt.osm.pbf). The bundled `data/parks.json` contains 7,099 park and plaza polygons, 22,054 mapped paths and 13,504 mapped tree points; the renderer samples up to 4,000 trees. © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). OpenStreetMap data is available under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). `data/parks.json` is a derivative database and is distributed under ODbL 1.0.
- **Land and streets:** NYC Department of City Planning borough boundaries, from [dwillis/nyc-maps](https://github.com/dwillis/nyc-maps/blob/master/boroughs.geojson), and geographic street centerlines mirrored in [fillerwriter/nyc-streets](https://github.com/fillerwriter/nyc-streets/blob/master/nyc-streets.geojson). Centerline fields follow US Census TIGER/Line. Coastline vertices are simplified to roughly 5 m spacing. This is a historical snapshot, not a current tide line.
- **Pigeon model:** The CC0 Blender source, `assets/pigeon/source.blend`, is [Low poly 3D Pigeon model (rigged + animated)](https://opengameart.org/content/low-poly-3d-pigeon-model-rigged-animated-untextured) by mujtaba-io, downloaded and supplied by the user. `assets/pigeon/pigeon.glb` contains the browser-ready rigged adaptation: a smoothed silhouette, vertex-colored plumage, amber eyes, adapted wing-flap/idle/leg-tuck motion, and added walking, pecking and folded-wing poses. Reproduce it with Blender using `blender -b assets/pigeon/source.blend --python tools/prepare-pigeon.py`. The adapted model remains CC0.
- **Three.js:** Vendored v0.180.0, MIT license (see `vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt`).
- **Photographic surface materials:** CC0 ambientCG brick, concrete, metal, asphalt, turf and soil maps, obtained from the packs mirrored in [fps-buildings-env-kit](https://github.com/petroulacl/fps-buildings-env-kit). Downsized originals, derived facade atlases, IDs and licenses are recorded in [assets/facades/README.txt](assets/facades/README.txt) and [assets/ground/CREDITS.md](assets/ground/CREDITS.md). `tools/prepare-facade-textures.py` rebuilds the facade atlases offline. Textures represent material families, not the actual facade imagery of each address.
- **Foliage and bark:** Leaf atlases by Daniel Greenheck from [EZ-Tree](https://github.com/dgreenheck/ez-tree), MIT; oak bark derives from Poly Haven's CC0 Bark Brown 02. See [assets/trees/CREDITS.md](assets/trees/CREDITS.md) and the bundled MIT license. Branching tree shapes are generated by Birdway, with textured foliage cards and bark; they are not scanned individual NYC trees. Species forms approximate plane, oak, honeylocust, linden, maple and conifer canopies; most source points identify leaf type rather than species.

The playable building and OSM subset covers Manhattan south of an approximate line along 59th Street. Neighboring shorelines provide distant context without full building coverage. Survey matching cannot represent every demolished or rebuilt building. Current construction, roof setbacks, façade materials, roads, park surfaces and landmark structures are simplified or stylized. One World Trade Center, landmark crowns, the Brooklyn Bridge, Washington Square Arch and High Line walkway include hand-built approximations.

To regenerate the data, `tools/prepare-map.py` takes a footprint payload, borough GeoJSON and streets GeoJSON; `tools/prepare-detail.py` matches the 2014 roof model and 2017 PLUTO metadata; `tools/prepare-parks.py` extracts OSM features from a Manhattan PBF. The first script uses only the Python standard library. The detail script requires NumPy and pyproj; the OSM script requires osmium. Source datasets retain their original terms; attribution does not imply endorsement by NYC or OpenStreetMap contributors.
